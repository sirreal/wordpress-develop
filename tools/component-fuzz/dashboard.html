Coverage Notes

Area Current Coverage Intentional Boundaries
Unicode email Verified 110,050 passed checks at 100 focused iterations. No mail delivery; validation/sanitization and user lookup behavior only.
Admin/browser flows Covered through direct helpers, list tables, dashboard, user preferences, AJAX helpers. Full browser page dispatch, redirects, and process exits are skipped.
